## 4. Interview one-pager

**Five angles** (verbatim substance from `book/PRESS-KIT.md`): the Aquinas angle; the genealogy angle;
the honesty angle (reject seven times out of fourteen); the pastoral angle (ninety seconds, one page,
three colours, one rule about the bedside); the falsifiability angle (ch. 36 explains how to prove the
book wrong).

**Seven questions the book is built to answer** (verbatim from the press kit): Why is "your thoughts
create reality" attractive to people who have left the Church? · What does Aquinas's method actually
consist of, and where does the analogy break down? · You accept four of the movement's claims — which
four, and why is that the harder position? · Which of the fourteen verdicts was hardest to write? ·
What should a priest say in ninety seconds to someone who asks whether manifesting is a sin? · Why
does the book say it has no authority, and does that not undercut it? · What would count as evidence
that you are wrong?

**Safe facts to state on the record:** 314 pages · 75,803 words · 40 chapters · 8 appendices (A–H) ·
first edition MMXXVI / 2026 · Clausery · Kidus Yared · free-to-read PDF, EPUB, reading edition · all rights reserved · Crimson Text
11/15 on 5.5 × 8.5 in · 14 claims / 14 verdicts (4 integrate, 3 correct, 7 reject) · 40 disputed
questions · 30-day pilot with controls · `urn:uuid:8f3c1a52-6b64-5d1e-9a77-2f4e0c9b7d31`.

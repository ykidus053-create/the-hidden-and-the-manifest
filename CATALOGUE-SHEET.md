> *The Hidden and the Manifest* does with the manifest space what Aquinas did with Aristotle — states it in

**Catalogue abstract (~90 words)**

> The manifestation movement is not a set of false beliefs but a set of true observations attached to a
> false engine. This study describes the movement in its own terms, dates its seven rules and their authors,
> and tests fourteen central claims with Aquinas's method: four to integrate, three to correct, seven to
> reject. It then proposes six marks of a Catholic practice, four transpositions of methods people already
> use, a nine-day novena, a one-page pastoral triage card and a thirty-day pilot with controls. Forty
> disputed questions remain open. A theological research project, not an authoritative text: no *imprimatur*
> has been sought or granted, and it is submitted to the Church for correction. © 2026 Kidus Yared. All
> rights reserved.

## 6. Five angles for an editor (verbatim from the press kit)

1. **The Aquinas angle.** A thirteenth-century method applied to a twenty-first-century feed, with the four limits of the comparison stated rather than hidden.
2. **The genealogy angle.** Manifestation looks perennial; the book dates it — Quimby's clinic, Evans's *The Mental Cure* (1869), the name "New Thought" in the 1890s, Hopkins, Eddy, the business wing, Neville, *The Secret*. Every rule has an author and a year.
3. **The honesty angle.** The verdict on the movement is *reject* seven times out of fourteen.
4. **The pastoral angle.** Ninety seconds, one page, three colours, one rule about the bedside: printable and usable.
5. **The falsifiability angle.** Chapter 36 tells the reader how to prove the book wrong — a thirty-day pilot, a control group, fruit rather than testimonials.

**Interview questions the book is built to answer** (verbatim): Why is "your thoughts create reality" attractive to people who have left the Church? · What does Aquinas's method actually consist of, and where does the Aristotle analogy break down? · You accept four of the movement's claims — which four, and why is that the harder position? · Which of the fourteen verdicts was hardest to write? · What should a priest say in ninety seconds to someone who asks whether manifesting is a sin? · Why does the book say it has no authority — and does that not undercut it? · What would count as evidence that you are wrong?

## 10. What not to say in a pitch (verbatim from `book/PRESS-KIT.md`)

- Not "the Catholic Church teaches" — the book is a research project, not a teaching document, and it says so on its copyright page.
- Not "debunks manifestation" — the book opens by granting four of its claims.
- Not "bestselling" or "acclaimed": there are no reviews yet, and a pitch that invents one is dead on arrival.
- Not "the only Catholic book on manifestation" — it is the most useful one *for this argument*, which is a claim that can be defended.

And from `book/MARKETING.md` §10: **do not market it as authoritative, and do not let anyone else market it
that way either.** Its credibility is that it refuses the imprimatur it does not have and asks to be
corrected in public.
